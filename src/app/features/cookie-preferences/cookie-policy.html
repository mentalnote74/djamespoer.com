Cookie Policy

djamespoer.com uses a single first-party preference cookie for this feature to remember your cookie-banner choice and playful cookie preferences. This preference cookie is not used for advertising, profiling or cross-site tracking.

What it remembers

The cookie, portfolio_cookie_preferences, stores a format version and your selected cookie varieties. A valid saved choice also tells the site you already answered. It contains no name, contact information or personal identifier. The cookie names are just playful preferences, not technical consent categories.

How long it lasts

It expires 180 days after you save a choice. Saving again restarts that period. It is scoped to this site's hostname and pages, not other sites, and uses a secure connection on HTTPS.

Change or delete it

Use Cookie settings in the footer to change your choices. Reject all saves an empty selection and still remembers that you answered. To delete the preference entirely, use your browser's cookie or site-data controls. Deleting it, expiry or a future incompatible preference format can bring the prompt back.

Separate analytics

The public site also uses Google Analytics, which may set separate analytics cookies. The playful selections here do not enable, disable or provide consent for analytics.

Cookie Policy

djamespoer.com uses a single first-party preference cookie for this feature to remember your cookie-banner choice and playful cookie preferences. This preference cookie is not used for advertising, profiling or cross-site tracking.

What it remembers

The cookie, portfolio_cookie_preferences, stores a format version and your selected cookie varieties. A valid saved choice also tells the site you already answered. It contains no name, contact information or personal identifier. The cookie names are just playful preferences, not technical consent categories.

How long it lasts

It expires 180 days after you save a choice. Saving again restarts that period. It is scoped to this site's hostname and pages, not other sites, and uses a secure connection on HTTPS.

Change or delete it

Use the Cookie Jar in the footer to change your choices. Saving an empty selection still remembers that you answered. To delete the preference entirely, use your browser's cookie or site-data controls. Deleting it, expiry or a future incompatible preference format can bring the prompt back.

Rigby and the Cookie Jar

Rigby is optional and off until you choose to play. The browser's local storage entry rigby.enabled.v1 remembers whether you turned him on or off on this device. You can change this in the Cookie Jar or Rigby's controls. It stays until you clear this site's data or change your choice. If storage is unavailable, your choice lasts for the current page visit. This setting does not change analytics consent.

The separate local storage entry rigby.cookie-jar.v1 remembers the identifiers of cookies you collected in the optional game. It contains no name or contact details. Progress persists on this device until you clear it. Use “Clear cookies and restart” inside the Cookie Jar to empty the game jar without changing your Rigby choice, favorite flavors or analytics settings. If saving is blocked, progress is kept only for the current page visit.

After you explore the page, Rigby may introduce himself once. The local storage entry rigby.greeting.seen.v1 remembers that introduction on this device so it does not repeat automatically. Turning Rigby off prevents the automatic greeting. The introduction does not start the hunt until you choose to play. Clear this site's data to remove this marker.

Optional analytics and internal browsers

Analytics is enabled when no choice is saved. A saved denial keeps collection off until you change it in the Cookie Jar. The separate first-party portfolio_analytics_consent cookie stores only granted or denied for 180 days (Path=/, SameSite=Lax, Secure on HTTPS). Changing your choice reloads the page to stop or start collection. Revoking consent prevents future collection; it cannot retract events already sent. Existing Google Analytics cookies may remain until cleared with browser site-data controls.

The reversible internal-browser setting uses the existing portfolio.analytics.internal local-storage marker. Marked browsers suppress page views and custom analytics even when analytics is allowed. It lasts until unmarked or site data is cleared and applies only to this browser profile and origin. No identity or experiment assignment is stored. The Rigby experiment is inactive.

Separate analytics

The public site uses Google Analytics by default, unless you save a denial in the Cookie Jar. You can change that choice at any time. Google Analytics may set separate analytics cookies. The playful selections here do not enable, disable or provide consent for analytics.

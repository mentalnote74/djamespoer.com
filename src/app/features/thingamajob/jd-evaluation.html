How your data is handled

Thingamajob sends the job text and James's public career facts to OpenAI. Private career documents are never included. Screenshots are sent to OpenAI only to read the job text; the pictures themselves are not kept. Submissions, answers and usage records are kept privately for 90 days for troubleshooting and improvement. A random 30-minute session links your answer and résumé download. Unless you have turned analytics off in the Cookie Jar, the full job text, the job title, the company and James's fit score are also sent to Google Analytics, with contact lines, email addresses, phone numbers and links removed first. File names are never sent.

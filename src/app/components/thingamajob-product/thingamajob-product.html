Thingamajob

An owner-controlled career platform, built from source-backed evidence.

A reusable product for turning scattered career material into reviewed evidence that can support a job search.

The problem

Career evidence is fragmented across résumés, LinkedIn, portfolios, documents, memory and job-search activity. That makes it difficult to distinguish an accurate, supported statement from an attractive but unsupported narrative.

The idea

Give the owner a reusable evidence foundation. Keep source material, proposed statements, review decisions and open questions connected, while leaving approval in human hands.

Thingamajob in use

This is the existing Thingamajob review component, presented with a fictional sample. Try Accept, Reject or Correct, inspect the result and history, then reset to explore another decision.

How it works

  1. Bring available career evidence together and retain its source.
  2. Identify possible facts and questions that still need an answer.
  3. Let the owner review, correct, accept or reject proposed statements.
  4. Reuse reviewed evidence while preserving its history.
  5. Extend that foundation toward evaluating opportunities, preparing source-grounded materials and feeding useful outcomes back into the job search.

Evidence, owner review and explicit question resolution are implemented in the protected prototype. Opportunity evaluation and material generation remain product direction; they are not offered by this walkthrough.

Human + AI operating model

Automation can propose; it cannot grant itself owner authority. Accepting or correcting a statement is an explicit human decision. Answering a question does not automatically resolve it. When an approved fact is corrected later, the original approval remains in history while the replacement becomes current.

The public walkthrough makes no AI calls. Its review actions are local interactions with fictional material, not authenticated decisions on a real person's records.

Prototype and evolution

The protected workflow supports evidence, proposals, owner review, reviewed statements, open questions and explicit resolution. It also preserves the history of corrections to previously approved facts. The walkthrough focuses on proposal review, using the same component and review controls with an isolated local adapter.

Selections and decisions in this walkthrough exist only in the current tab. Reset or reload restores the sample. It does not upload files, access a private workspace or persist a real career fact.

Design, accessibility and engineering

The Angular feature owns its presentation state behind a service boundary. The protected application obtains identity and ownership decisions from the server. The portfolio supplies a separate, tab-local adapter; it has no private API connection and receives no client records.

Native labels, fieldsets, buttons and disclosure controls support keyboard operation. Review outcomes receive a focused status message. Source text and proposed wording render as data, never executable markup. The case study uses the portfolio's shared main landmark, navigation and lazy routing.

Iteration has moved the feature from a placeholder to a protected owner workflow, added history-preserving correction, and now made the real review UI inspectable inside this case study.

Relationship to Daily Job Dredge

Thingamajob is the reusable career platform. Daily Job Dredge is a separate operational workflow for finding and processing opportunities. Opportunities can inform evidence-based evaluation, and reviewed outcomes can inform later searches. That is an intended interface between the systems, not a claim that a live integration is already connected here.

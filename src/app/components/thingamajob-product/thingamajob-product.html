Thingamajob

An owner-controlled career platform built from source-backed evidence.

The case study

The problem

Career evidence is scattered across résumés, profiles, documents and memory. Unsupported wording can sound convincing without being accurate.

The idea

Build a reusable foundation that connects source material, suggested statements, human decisions and open questions.

How it works

Collect evidence, identify facts and questions, let the owner review wording, then reuse the supported statements. The local walkthrough highlights job-text overlap with fictional evidence; it does not assess overall suitability.

Human + AI operating model

Automation can suggest; the owner decides. An answer does not automatically resolve a question, and a suggestion does not become an approved fact. This walkthrough makes no AI calls.

Independent clients

James is Client #1, the first live prototype. Erica is Client #2, the second independent live prototype. The shared platform keeps their evidence and workspaces separate; neither person's records are used here.

Prototype and evolution

The protected prototype supports evidence, review, explicit question resolution and history-preserving correction. The public surface reuses its review component with fictional, tab-local data. Full opportunity evaluation and application generation remain product direction.

Design, accessibility and engineering

Angular owns presentation state behind a service boundary. The protected server owns identity and authority. Native controls, keyboard focus, responsive layout and escaped text support accessible review without exposing private APIs.

Relationship to Daily Job Dredge

Daily Job Dredge is a separate opportunity-finding workflow. It can inform evidence-based evaluation and learn from outcomes; no live integration is connected in this walkthrough.

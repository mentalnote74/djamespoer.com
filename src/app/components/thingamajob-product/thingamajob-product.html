A career assistant built around evidence

The problem

Résumés are snapshots. They leave things out, compress years of work into a few bullets, and often fail to describe the evidence behind a candidate’s actual experience.

Traditional job matching usually makes that worse. It compares job-description language to résumé language and rewards phrase overlap.

That creates two bad outcomes:

  • real experience can be missed because the wording is different
  • AI can be tempted to fill gaps by guessing

Thingamajob starts somewhere else: with evidence.

The Library of Truth

Thingamajob builds a Library of Truth from the career evidence a person actually has.

That evidence can include historical résumés, project and case-study material, public professional pages, career documents, owner-supplied context, and other relevant evidence.

AI can inspect that material, extract candidate claims, connect related evidence, and surface uncertainty.

But AI does not decide what becomes truth.

The person reviews proposed claims and can accept them, correct them, reject them, or add context.

AI may propose. Evidence supports. The person decides.

Provenance matters

A claim is useful only if Thingamajob can explain where it came from.

Extracted claims retain links back to supporting evidence so the owner can inspect the source instead of trusting a black-box summary.

Uncertainty is preserved rather than silently rewritten. Conflicting evidence can remain visible until the person resolves it.

The first proof

The first formal Thingamajob proof uses Client #1—James himself.

Thingamajob independently reconstructs a Library of Truth from ordinary client evidence rather than being given the already-reconciled answer key.

That independently built LoT can later be compared against a sealed benchmark to measure what was recovered, missed, unsupported, ambiguous, or unavailable from the supplied evidence.

This separates extraction quality from owner-assisted correction.

From evidence to job evaluation

Once the Library of Truth is established, Thingamajob evaluates job requirements against verified career evidence.

Jobs can enter through: - pasted job-description text - a job-posting URL - a supported uploaded file

Those inputs normalize into one evaluation flow.

Instead of inventing a match percentage, requirements use four states:

PROVEN Direct evidence supports the requirement.

SUPPORTED Related evidence supports the requirement, but not as strongly or directly.

CONFIRMATION NEEDED The evidence suggests a possible match, but the person needs to clarify it.

GAP The current Library of Truth does not support the requirement.

The goal is explainable fit, not keyword theater.

Human control

Thingamajob is intentionally human-in-the-loop.

AI can extract requirements, search evidence, propose relationships, explain relevance, and draft useful outputs.

The person controls truth.

Multi-client by design

Thingamajob is one reusable product, not a separate application for every candidate.

Each client’s workspace is designed to keep their evidence, Library of Truth, preferences, evaluations, review history, generated materials, and career identity separate.

One client’s evidence must never leak into another client’s workspace.

James is Client #1. Erica Kortz is Client #2.

Designed for failure too

If AI capacity, provider quota, or POC budget becomes unavailable, user work remains preserved, paid calls do not retry endlessly, recovery stays obvious, and billing/provider internals remain hidden from normal users.

Running out of AI capacity may pause intelligence, but it must never strand the user in a broken interface.

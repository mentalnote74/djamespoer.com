Thingamajob

A reusable career platform built around source-backed evidence.

Thingamajob connects career evidence with the work of exploring roles, preparing application materials and managing a job search. It starts with what a person can substantiate, rather than generating a career story without sources.

Evidence before assertions

The working foundation lets an owner review evidence-backed proposals, accept, reject or correct them, and explicitly resolve open questions. An automated suggestion does not become an approved career fact on its own. Original sources and decision history remain available to the owner.

One foundation, a broader career workflow

The platform is designed to support:

  • Career evidence: organize source material and reviewed career facts.
  • Role evaluation: relate a person's evidence to a role's requirements.
  • Application materials: prepare source-grounded résumés and application content.
  • Job-search workflow: connect opportunities, decisions and follow-up.

Evidence and owner review form the working foundation. Role evaluation, application materials and the broader job-search workflow describe the product direction, not actions offered on this page.

Two independent prototypes

James is Client #1, the first live prototype. Erica is Client #2, the second independent live prototype. They use the same reusable product approach, with separate career profiles and owner-controlled evidence. One person's career facts are not another person's content.

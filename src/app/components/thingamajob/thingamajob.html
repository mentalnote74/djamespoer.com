Thingamajob

I’m building a career assistant that evaluates jobs against verified career evidence instead of guessing from résumé keywords.

Thingamajob starts with a Library of Truth built from real career evidence—résumés, project work, public professional material, and owner-reviewed claims. AI can extract, connect, and explain evidence, but the person controls what becomes truth.

Job evaluations are designed around four states:

  • Proven
  • Supported
  • Confirmation needed
  • Gap

The goal is simple: make job matching more accurate, more explainable, and less dependent on résumé keyword theater.

Thingamajob is an active product that continues to evolve through real job-search workflows.

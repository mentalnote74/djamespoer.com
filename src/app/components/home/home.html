Pardon the dust.

You're standing in the middle of a working construction site. On purpose.

Most portfolios show you the finished house after the tools, sawdust, mistakes, experiments, and bad ideas have been swept away.

I'm doing the opposite.

djamespoer.com is being designed and built in public. The unfinished pieces are part of the portfolio: UX decisions, accessibility work, Angular architecture, experiments, failures, fixes, testing, AI-assisted development—and the occasional spectacularly bad idea that gets replaced by a better one.

Some rooms are finished. Some still have studs showing. Some may change while you're standing in them.

That's the point. You're not just looking at what I built. You're watching how I build.

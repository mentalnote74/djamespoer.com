Methodology / evidence boundaries

Project completion and committed scope

{{ data.metrics.completion.reason }}

{{ data.supportingFacts.scope }}

Project points come from an owner-approved, Git-versioned item-level baseline and append-only observations. Protected standalone Thingamajob and Erica work, samples, duplicate records, superseded scope and epic rollups are excluded. Burndown shows observed remaining points from T₀ onward; no historical trajectory or ideal line is invented. Scope records additions, removals and estimate changes separately. The initial observation is one point, not a trend. Updates require an approved observation; page loads do not advance the observation date. Jira inventory and throughput retain their separate source dates.

Velocity, throughput and history

{{ data.metrics.velocity.reason }}

Throughput counts historical eligible non-Done to Done transitions, even when the issue is now open. Repeated Done entries do not count again without an evidenced reopen. Seven-day counts and 14-day charts end at the source capture timestamp; buckets are open on the start and closed on the end. No resolution-date fallback invents a completion event.

Production deployment history

Unique successful production activation records in the seven days ending at {{ data.supportingFacts.shippingCheckedAt }}. Preview attempts, failures and non-deployed builds are excluded. History is retained evidence, not guaranteed complete lifetime coverage.

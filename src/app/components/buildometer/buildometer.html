Methodology / know what the instruments know.

Completion denominator

Done committed items divided by all active committed items, multiplied by 100 and rounded to a whole percent. Uncommitted backlog, cancelled and superseded items are excluded. Scope changes require a new version and separate reporting. No committed scope snapshot is connected yet.

Deployment freshness and journal publication dates are separate. Journal notes are qualitative evidence; they do not establish completion, velocity or bug rates. Missing metrics stay unmeasured until their source is connected.

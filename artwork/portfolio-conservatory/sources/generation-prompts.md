# Built-in imagegen lineage

## Concept master

Use case: illustration-story. Create ONE original, extraordinarily art-directed panoramic website carousel artwork, 3:1 aspect ratio, about 2172x724. A distinctive visual invitation for djamespoer.com, a creative UX engineer's portfolio. The graphic earns a click through beauty, curiosity, material richness and surprise. It does not explain UX research, illustrate a workflow, or tell a case-study story. No text, lettering, numbers, logos or watermark.

Concept: THE IMPOSSIBLE CONSERVATORY. A luxuriant, inhabitable architectural daydream, intimate and intriguing rather than a monumental generic fantasy building. A sculptural pavilion carved from warm shell-colored limestone and honeyed wood stands over very dark, almost still reflective water. Its structure folds into an unexpected but elegant arrangement of organic vaulted openings, curved clear glass rooms, slender warm copper mullions, terraced planted niches and one graceful stair that disappears through an opening. Roots and finely detailed lush foliage thread through the architecture, with a few richly textured broad leaves close to camera. A small extraordinary translucent botanical canopy catches the light like delicate hand-blown glass, while most plants are convincingly organic. Carefully composed wildness against beautifully precise crafted surfaces. Beautiful tactile grain, veins in stone, delicately rippled reflections, leaf shadows and glimpses of mysterious rooms. The cool factor comes from the discovery of an impossible yet welcoming place with exquisite visual craft.

Composition: wide 3:1 frame. Left 34 percent deliberately dark quiet atmospheric space for semantic HTML copy, gently connected to the scene by reflected light. One clear asymmetrical architectural/garden silhouette occupies the right 65 percent. Close enough to see crafted details and get a strong immediate read at carousel scale. Avoid a distant tiny building or sprawling skyline. Main focal opening around 66 percent across, 47 percent down: a broad irregular limestone arch framing a luminous deeper garden room. At least three other NATURAL apertures branch through the structure at different depths: a narrow vertical glass opening, a low horizontal water-level passage, a gap between two planted terraces. These spaces should clearly reveal deeper room/water/light planes so they can be separated for browser composition. Foreground leaf and stone edges partially occlude deeper scenery. Dense reward for looking longer, disciplined focal hierarchy and strong negative space. Fit main silhouette comfortably within canvas.

Palette and lighting: stable materials in sand, shell, driftwood, restrained copper, deep sea-green foliage and ink-blue water. Soft warm low-angle light paired with cooler water reflections. Sky is a restrained luminous atmospheric gradient, not a literal sunset beach photograph. Light should travel through natural openings and bounce on water and glass. Plenty of rich shadow to make illuminated surfaces sing; avoid uniformly beige or muddy rendering. Detailed cinematic editorial artwork with painterly natural atmosphere and physically convincing architecture, not generic shiny game concept art. No coral/teal paper-model look, sterile showroom, chrome abstraction, spaceship, flying craft, weapons, steampunk machine, diagram, dashboard, floating UI cards, people explaining anything, tropical resort, palm trees, cocktails or beach props. Do not imitate Igloo Inc, Shopify Editions, Immersive Garden or another existing site's composition, symbols, typography or signature imagery. Invent an original composition in djamespoer.com's material/environmental vocabulary.

This is the concept master for a runtime-composited scene, not a monolithic final runtime asset. Make the near stone/foliage silhouette and the through-openings crisp enough to separate later; deep atmosphere, reflections and obscured rooms may be softer. Keep the main arch, translucent canopy and surprising stair concentrated together so a closer mobile composition can retain the strongest visual hook.

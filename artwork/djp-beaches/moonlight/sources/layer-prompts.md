# Concept

Create a breathtaking panoramic 3:1 photographic fine-art beach scene for djamespoer.com, world-class immersive website artwork. Transport the viewer standing at the edge of an impossibly beautiful remote tropical cove at full moon. This is its own composition, not a blue-filter sunset. Low viewpoint, crystalline cobalt lagoon with intense but natural electric cyan bioluminescence tracing several irregular breaking wave edges and tiny luminous plankton caught in shallow tide pools. Silver-white sand curves from lower right into middle distance, sculptural black basalt rocks in lower right and a dark dramatic karst island at far left. Refined moonlit palm foliage frames upper right with open gaps between fronds. A bright realistic full moon of modest size above right-center, luminous silver path crossing the water toward viewer, thin translucent ragged clouds partly veil its halo. Deep indigo sky, sparse fine stars; a distant violet-blue atmospheric horizon, rich darkness and beautiful precise highlights. Sublime photographic material realism, extraordinary depth, luminous water, sophisticated restrained color, cinematic but a real place someone longs to enter. Left center open darker sea for later HTML title, do not put text in image. Avoid people (recruiter is viewer), buildings, resort furniture, boats, spacecraft, fantasy castles, giant planets, neon ribbons, circular portals, logos, typography. The turquoise glow lives in natural foam and moving seawater only, with dark intervals. Foreground rocks and palm framing are clearly separable silhouette layers with natural openings showing water and sky. Landscape panoramic aspect ratio 3:1.
